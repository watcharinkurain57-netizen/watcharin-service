# Creative Campus / Homepage direction

## Brief

Rebuild the homepage as a personal portfolio for Watcharin Kurain. Keep the approved interactive 3D campus. Translate the observed Tengile MalaMala principles—large subject-led hero, generous composition, restrained image parallax, side-panel navigation and a curtain transition—into Watcharin's own visual language.

## Tokens

- Campus mist `#e1e6da`: quiet hero background derived from the campus trees and ground.
- Paper `#f5f2e9`: reading and project sections.
- Forest `#243d32`: text, navigation and closing invitation.
- Stone `#5b665c`: supporting copy.
- Clay `#bc714a`: existing campus roof / interaction accent.
- Line `#c9d0c3`: separators where they describe navigation or hierarchy.
- Inter: large English title, project names and navigation. IBM Plex Sans Thai: Thai reading and headings. Retain the existing licensed font delivery.

## Composition

Desktop: a quiet full-width header, a large two-line title to the left of the 3D world, and a compact scene caption. Mobile: title and introduction first, then the world with usable building controls.

## Brief review

The distinctive element is the functioning campus, not an ornamental typeface or a grid of identical service cards. Keep the established low-poly assets; give the real brand photo its own composition. Avoid statistics, testimonials or implied client outcomes. Distinguish the website concept image from the real WANSABYE photo. The other three zones remain readable links, not repeated marketing cards.

## Motion

One camera introduction per session; replay and skip controls remain available. Scroll reveals are reserved for grouped narrative content. Images move by at most 3% inside their frames; native scrolling stays intact. User-triggered navigation uses a short forest curtain. Side-panel menu items clarify in a sequence. Reduced-motion preference bypasses camera journeys, parallax, curtain and menu animations. Scene rendering pauses outside the viewport and when the tab is hidden.

## Acceptance

Visually inspect desktop and mobile, keyboard menu / Escape / focus return, actual zone and work navigation, browser Back, reduced motion and poster fallback. Run TypeScript, scoped ESLint and production build. Keep existing contact, route and workspace access behavior.
